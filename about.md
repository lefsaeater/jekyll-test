## The short version

I am **lefsaeater**, and I use this space to think in public about thoughtful work, useful technology, and the small details that shape our days.

The site is deliberately simple. There are no trackers, pop-ups, or complicated publishing systems. It is built from plain text with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) and hosted on GitHub Pages.
